About Us

Built for owners who expect more than a contractor

Ankorius was founded to solve one problem: managing a property shouldn’t mean juggling a dozen different suppliers. We bring cleaning, maintenance, electrical and waste services together under one team, one standard, and one accountable point of contact.

We work with property owners, investors and portfolio managers who need reliability they can measure — not promises. Every engagement is scoped around your asset, your tenants and your timeline.

  • Insured, vetted service teams on every site
  • Clear, transparent reporting on every visit
  • Fast, dependable response when it matters
