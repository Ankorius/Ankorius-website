Buildings with a fire alarm system need it tested regularly, and the results recorded. Ankorius carries out scheduled fire alarm testing and keeps a clear log of every test, result and follow-up action, so you have the records ready for audits, insurers and inspections.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is responsible for fire alarm testing?

Under fire safety law, the responsible person for a building, usually the owner, landlord or managing agent, must make sure fire safety measures, including the alarm, are maintained. Regular testing and good records help you show that this has been done.

How often should a fire alarm be tested?

Guidance generally recommends a regular routine, often a weekly test of the system, alongside periodic servicing by a competent engineer. The right routine depends on your building and system, so check your fire risk assessment and ask us if you are unsure.

Do you repair or install fire alarm systems?

Our service covers testing and logging. Servicing, repair and installation of fire alarm systems must be carried out by suitably qualified engineers. If we find a fault, we record it and report it so that it can be put right.
