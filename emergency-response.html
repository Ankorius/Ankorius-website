Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the emergency line without a contract?

No. 24/7 emergency response is for clients on a contract with Ankorius. Get in touch to discuss a contract that includes emergency cover.

What counts as an emergency?

Typically an urgent problem that cannot wait until normal working hours, such as a leak or burst pipe. The scope is agreed in your contract.

What are your normal working hours?

Our usual hours are Monday to Sunday, 8am to 6pm. Out-of-hours emergency cover applies to contracted clients.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in London and mainly cover East London and the surrounding areas. Get in touch with your property addresses and we will confirm what is possible.

How do I arrange cover?

Contact us using the form, by phone or email, or on WhatsApp, and we will discuss a contract that suits your properties.

Tell us about your portfolio and we will discuss a contract.
