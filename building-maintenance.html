Reactive and planned maintenance covering the fabric of your building, inside and out.

Keep your building in good order

Ankorius provides reactive repairs and planned maintenance for property owners, landlords and managers in East London and the surrounding areas. We keep the fabric of your building in good condition, so small problems are resolved before they become costly ones.

As with our cleaning, you get one accountable team instead of coordinating several contractors. We agree the scope with you, carry out the work and keep you informed.

  • Reactive repairs when problems arise
  • Planned maintenance to prevent problems
  • Interior and exterior building fabric

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between reactive and planned maintenance?

Reactive maintenance deals with problems as they arise. Planned maintenance is scheduled in advance to keep the building in good condition and avoid problems in the first place. Many clients use a mix of both.

Do you carry out gas or electrical work?

Gas, electrical and other regulated work must be done by suitably qualified, registered engineers. Tell us what you need and we will advise on how it can be handled.

Can you decorate between tenancies?

Yes. We can fill, sand and repaint walls and repair minor damage, so the property is ready for the next occupant.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in London and mainly cover East London and the surrounding areas. Get in touch with the property address and we will confirm that we can help.

How do I get a quote?

Describe the work using the contact form, by phone or email, or on WhatsApp, and we will send you a proposal.
