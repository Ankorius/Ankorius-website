Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how the Ankorius website (operated by Ankorius Limited, company number 17454633) uses cookies and similar technologies, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) and UK GDPR.

In short: this website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking technologies. That is why you are not shown a cookie banner.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work, remember your preferences, measure how a site is used, or show advertising. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, work in comparable ways.

2. Cookies we use

At the date of this policy, the Ankorius website does not set or read any cookies, local storage or similar identifiers on your device. In particular, we do not use:

  • analytics or statistics tools on this website (such as Google Analytics);
  • advertising, retargeting or social media tracking pixels; or
  • any cookies for profiling or marketing.

Because we only use technologies that are strictly necessary to deliver the page you asked for, PECR does not require us to obtain your consent, so we do not display a cookie banner.

3. Third-party services on our site

Some features load content from, or send information to, third parties. Based on our current setup, these do not set cookies on our website, but they do receive limited technical information such as your IP address when your browser contacts them:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) — your browser downloads the typefaces used on this site directly from Google's servers.
  • Formspree (formspree.io) — if you submit our contact form, your message is sent to Formspree, which delivers it to us by email.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider, which keeps standard server logs.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you click the WhatsApp button, you leave our website and WhatsApp's own cookie and privacy policies apply.

You can read how we handle personal data in our Privacy Policy.

4. If this changes

If we later add analytics, advertising or any other non-essential cookies, we will first update this policy and ask for your consent through a cookie banner before any such cookies are set. You will be able to accept or refuse them, and to change your mind at any time.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

Although we do not set cookies, you can control cookies from any website through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Guidance for common browsers is available at aboutcookies.org. You can also find more information about cookies on the ICO website.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, email us at info@ankorius.com.
