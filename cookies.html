Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains how the Ankorius website (operated by Ankorius Limited, company number 17454633) uses cookies and similar technologies, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR) and UK GDPR.

In short: we only set analytics cookies (Google Analytics) if you click “Accept analytics” on our cookie banner. If you reject, no analytics cookies are set. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and you can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work, remember your preferences, measure how a site is used, or show advertising. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, work in comparable ways.

2. Cookies and similar technologies we use

Strictly necessary (always on)

We do not set any strictly necessary cookies. We do store a single entry in your browser’s local storage, named ankorius_cookie_consent, to remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics so we do not ask you again on every page. It contains only your choice and the date, is stored for up to 12 months, and is not shared with anyone. Storage that is strictly necessary to honour your privacy choice does not require consent under PECR.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click “Accept analytics”, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to understand how visitors use our website (for example which pages are viewed, how people arrive at the site, and the type of device and approximate location they use) so we can improve it. It sets the following first-party cookies:

  • _ga — distinguishes unique visitors by assigning a randomly generated identifier. Expires after 2 years.
  • _ga_<container-id> — used to persist session state. Expires after 2 years.

The information collected is pseudonymous, and we have turned off Google signals and advertising personalisation features. Google acts as our service provider (processor) for this analytics, and the data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries; see our Privacy Policy for details. If you reject analytics, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

What we do not use

  • advertising, retargeting or social media tracking pixels; or
  • any cookies for profiling or marketing.

3. Third-party services on our site

Some features load content from, or send information to, third parties. Based on our current setup, these do not set cookies on our website, but they do receive limited technical information such as your IP address when your browser contacts them:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) — your browser downloads the typefaces used on this site directly from Google's servers.
  • Formspree (formspree.io) — if you submit our contact form, your message is sent to Formspree, which delivers it to us by email.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider, which keeps standard server logs.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you click the WhatsApp button, you leave our website and WhatsApp's own cookie and privacy policies apply.

You can read how we handle personal data in our Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices

The first time you visit, a banner lets you accept or reject analytics cookies, and rejecting is as easy as accepting. We will not set analytics cookies unless you accept. You can change your mind at any time by clicking “Cookie settings” in the footer of any page; if you then reject, we stop analytics and remove the analytics cookies we have set. We will ask again after 12 months, or sooner if we change the cookies we use. If we ever add other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

You can also control cookies from any website through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Guidance for common browsers is available at aboutcookies.org. You can also find more information about cookies on the ICO website.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, email us at info@ankorius.com.
