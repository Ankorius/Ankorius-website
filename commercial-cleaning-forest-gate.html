Commercial cleaning for Forest Gate blocks, offices and shops

Forest Gate is a mix of converted houses, purpose-built blocks, small offices and shop units, many of them along Woodgrange Road and around Forest Gate station. Ankorius provides commercial cleaning across Forest Gate and the E7 area for landlords, managing agents and local businesses.
