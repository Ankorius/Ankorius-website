A reliable cleaning partner for your properties

Ankorius provides commercial cleaning and janitorial services for property managers, landlords and commercial premises in East London and the surrounding areas. Whether you need a regular schedule or a one-off clean, we work to a clear scope agreed with you, so you know what is being done and when.

One accountable team means you do not have to coordinate several suppliers. We tailor the work to your building, your occupants and your schedule.

  • Scheduled and on-demand cleaning
  • Common areas, offices and washrooms
  • Tailored to your building and occupants
  • Specialist and deep cleans, including high-level work

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer regular scheduled cleaning?

Yes. We provide scheduled cleaning on a routine agreed with you, as well as on-demand and one-off cleans.

Can you clean communal areas and bin stores?

Yes. We clean common areas, and we have cleared and tidied communal bin stores that had become overfull.

Do you do one-off deep cleans?

Yes. Deep cleans and one-off cleans are available, including properties that have been left in a poor condition.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in London and mainly cover East London and the surrounding areas. Get in touch with the property address and we will confirm that we can help.

How do I get a quote?

Send us the property details using the contact form, by phone or email, or on WhatsApp, and we will send you a proposal.
