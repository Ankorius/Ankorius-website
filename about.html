About Ankorius

Quality is the standard we hold ourselves to

We are an East London facility services company. We take real pride in the quality of our work, and we want you to see it every time we leave a building.

Who we are

Ankorius provides commercial cleaning, building maintenance, fire alarm testing and logging, and 24/7 emergency response for contracted clients. We work with property managers, landlords, owners and businesses, mainly across East London and the surrounding areas.

We started Ankorius because looking after a property should not mean coordinating several suppliers and checking up on each of them. You deal with one team, agree one clear scope, and expect one consistent standard. When the work is done, it should look done.

  • One accountable team for your property
  • A clear scope agreed before we start
  • Site audits and quality control checks
  • Straightforward, honest communication
