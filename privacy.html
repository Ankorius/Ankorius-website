Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal data Ankorius collects through this website and when you contact us, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under UK data protection law.

In short: we only collect what we need to respond to your enquiry and deliver our services. We never sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Ankorius Limited, trading as Ankorius ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England and Wales under company number 17454633.

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR") and the Data Protection Act 2018, we are the "data controller" of the personal data described in this policy.

You can contact us about this policy or your personal data at info@ankorius.com.

2. The personal data we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, phone number, email address, your role (for example property owner or manager), the service you are interested in, and any details you choose to include in your message about your property.
  • Email, phone and WhatsApp: if you contact us directly, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address, telephone number (including your WhatsApp number and profile name if you message us on WhatsApp) and the content of your messages.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested. This is used to deliver the site securely and reliably.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools on this website, and we do not ask for payment details, special category data or information about children. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your messages.

3. How and why we use your data

We only use your personal data when UK law allows us to. The purposes and our lawful bases are:

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a proposal or quote — this is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and is in our legitimate interests in running our business.
  • To provide our services and manage our relationship with you (for example scheduling, account management and invoicing) — this is necessary for the performance of a contract with you or your organisation.
  • To keep records and meet legal obligations (for example accounting and tax requirements) — this is necessary to comply with the law.
  • To keep our website and systems secure and working properly, and to prevent spam and misuse of our contact form — this is in our legitimate interests.
  • To establish, exercise or defend legal claims — this is in our legitimate interests.

We do not send marketing communications unless you have asked us to or have given your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Your data is used within Ankorius only for the purposes set out above.

To run our business we do use a small number of trusted service providers ("processors") who handle data on our behalf and only on our instructions. These currently include:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers messages submitted through our website contact form to our email inbox.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.
  • Google Fonts — supplies the typefaces used on this site. Your browser requests the font files directly from Google, which means your IP address is sent to Google when the page loads.
  • Our email and communications providers — used to send and receive the emails and messages you exchange with us.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — if you choose to message us on WhatsApp, your messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may also share personal data with our professional advisers (such as accountants, insurers and lawyers) where necessary, and with the police, courts, regulators or other authorities where we are legally required to. If we ever sell or restructure our business, personal data may be transferred to the new owner, who would be bound to use it in line with this policy.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our service providers, such as Formspree, Vercel and Google, are based in or process data in countries outside the UK, including the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure it is protected by an appropriate safeguard permitted under UK GDPR, such as the UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge where the recipient is certified) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. You can contact us for more information about these safeguards.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes it was collected for:

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, after which they are deleted.
  • Clients and contracts: for the duration of the contract and then for six years, to meet accounting, tax and limitation-period requirements.
  • Website server logs: for a short period determined by our hosting provider, normally no more than 30 days.

We may keep data for longer where the law requires it or where it is needed to deal with a legal claim.

7. How we keep your data secure

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections on this website, access controls on our systems and limiting access to those who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will act promptly if something goes wrong and will notify you and the Information Commissioner's Office where the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it;
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances;
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances;
  • Portability — receive data you gave us in a structured, commonly used format where we process it by automated means on the basis of consent or contract;
  • Object to our use of your data where we rely on legitimate interests, and to any direct marketing; and
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on your consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@ankorius.com. We may need to verify your identity first. We will respond within one month, and we do not normally charge a fee.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Cookies

This website does not currently set any cookies. For details, please see our Cookie Policy.

10. Links to other websites

This website may link to third-party sites and services, such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

11. Children

Our website and services are directed at businesses, property owners and property managers. They are not intended for anyone under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we change the tools we use or the law changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. We encourage you to check back periodically.
